Materials You Will Need

To make a good kite, you will need these simple items:

  • Thin bamboo sticks (for the frame)
  • Colorful kite paper
  • Strong cotton thread (Saddi)
  • Glue or tape
  • Scissors

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Cut the bamboo sticks to the correct size: one straight stick for the spine and one curved stick for the bow.
  2. Tie the two sticks together in the shape of a cross using your thread.
  3. Cut the colorful paper into a diamond shape, making it slightly bigger than your bamboo frame.
  4. Fold the edges of the paper over the thread outlining the frame and glue them down tightly.
  5. Attach a small paper triangle at the bottom for the tail to help balance the kite in the wind!
